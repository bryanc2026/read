## The AI upsell — and a repricing insight

Concept (Bryan's words): build custom tooling in Claude Code, customize it
per merchant, and teach the ecommerce managers/owners to use it so they're
more efficient and proactive. Access is tied to being a Boom member — if a
merchant leaves, they lose the tooling. This embeds Boom into the
merchant's operations (real lock-in / switching cost), which supports
charging materially more per merchant.

Bryan's own framing: **10 merchants at $5k/month ($50k/mo total) instead
of 20 merchants at $2k/month ($40k/mo total)** — same or better revenue,
half the service load.

**Why this matters more than it might first look:** Boom already has 10
active merchants today. If even a meaningful chunk of the *existing* base
could be moved onto a $5k/month AI-embedded tier, that alone could clear
the $20k/month-each target ($40k/mo combined) — **without landing a single
new merchant.** This reframes "we need more sales" — the faster lever
might be repricing/upgrading the current base, with new-merchant
acquisition (via Nathan Bush / Studio Almond / SEO) as the second, parallel
lever rather than the only one. See `open-questions.md` for what's needed
to validate this (how many of the current 10 are actually ICP-fit, and
what it takes operationally to deliver the AI tier).
