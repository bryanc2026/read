## Commercials

- Price: **$1,950 per merchant** (billing cadence not yet confirmed — see
  open questions).
- Current revenue: roughly **$8,000–$11,000/month each** (i.e. per partner —
  implies total business revenue roughly double that, ~$16k–$22k/month
  combined, but this hasn't been confirmed explicitly).
- Target: **$20,000/month each** — i.e., needs to roughly **double**
  current revenue.
- **Capacity: 10 active merchants, cap of 20.** So there is headroom to
  double merchant count before capacity becomes the constraint.
- **Retention: only the sub-$1M "bad fit" merchants have ever churned.**
  No $5M+/Shopify Plus (ICP) merchant has left so far. This isn't just a
  service-effort argument for focusing on the ICP — it's a retention/LTV
  argument too.

## The AI upsell — real numbers, and where it fits alongside new sales

Concept (Bryan's words): build custom tooling in Claude Code, customize it
per merchant, and teach the ecommerce managers/owners to use it so they're
more efficient and proactive. Access is tied to being a Boom member — if a
merchant leaves, they lose the tooling. This embeds Boom into the
merchant's operations (real lock-in / switching cost), which supports
charging materially more per merchant.

**Confirmed tier breakdown of the current 10 active merchants:**
- **Tier 1 — AI-ready, high confidence (Shopify Plus):** Alton Goods,
  Orange Sherbet, Roogenic, Elle & Riley. Estimated **+$2k–$3k/month on
  top of** the existing $1,950 base each (not a $5k flat replacement as
  first framed — additive).
- **Tier 2 — smaller, lower confidence but possibly willing to pay:**
  Hairgang, Millwood Shoes, Precision Tools, Odd Things, Heirest.
- **Status: still conceptual.** First real rollout is happening now —
  **Iain is building/launching it for Roogenic first**, as the pilot
  (#1 target).

**Rough math:** 4 Tier-1 merchants × ~$2.5k/mo add-on ≈ **+$10k/month
combined** if all 4 convert — roughly half the estimated combined-revenue
gap to the $20k/month-each target (current combined revenue ~$16k–22k/mo,
target ~$40k/mo combined). Tier 2 is upside on top of that, but unproven.

**Bryan's explicit correction, and the right way to hold both ideas at
once:** even with zero churn among ICP merchants so far, churn is
inevitable eventually (merchant gets acquired, budget cuts, strategy
change, etc.) — so the AI upsell is a genuine near-term revenue lever, but
it **cannot replace new-merchant acquisition** as an ongoing engine. Both
run in parallel:
1. **AI upsell to the existing base** (fast, high-trust, ~half the gap if
   Tier 1 converts) — proof point incoming via the Roogenic pilot.
2. **New merchant acquisition** (Nathan Bush re-engagement, Studio Almond,
   SEO) — necessary regardless, both for the rest of the gap and as
   insurance against future churn.
