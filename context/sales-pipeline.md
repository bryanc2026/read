# Sales & Referral Pipeline

*Last updated: 2026-09-18*

## Ideal Customer Profile (confirmed by Bryan)

The 3 best current merchants — **Alton Goods, Orange Sherbet, Roogenic** —
share:
- **$5M+ annual revenue**
- **Shopify Plus**
- Need strategy/guidance, then execute themselves. Low hand-holding.

## Anti-pattern (also confirmed)

Merchants under **$1M–$3M revenue**:
- Need much more hand-holding.
- More likely to drop out.
- Described by Bryan as "too emotional."
- Implication: these are lower LTV, higher churn, and cost more of Bryan's/
  Iain's time per dollar of revenue than the $5M+ ICP above.

## Channels tried that have NOT worked

- Facebook ads
- LinkedIn outreach offering 3 free consults
- Bryan's own words: "nothing has worked" on paid/outbound.

## Channels that ARE working

- **Referrals** — the majority of the list above, and the source of all 3
  best-fit ($5M+ Shopify Plus) merchants.
- **SEO / organic search** — closed 2 merchants (Roogenic, Hairgang), zero
  spend, and Bryan closes most leads that reach him regardless of source.

## Two repeat referral sources — worth understanding and nurturing

- **"Bushy"** — referred both Alton Goods AND Orange Sherbet, i.e. both of
  the top-tier $5M+ examples. Who is Bushy (merchant, advisor, industry
  contact)? This may be the single highest-leverage relationship in the
  whole pipeline.
- **Studio Almond** — referred both Odd Things and Heirest. Sounds like a
  design/dev agency or similar partner. Worth understanding the
  relationship and whether it can be formalized/expanded.

## Pricing note

Standard price is $1,950/merchant, but **Heirest is on a $5k/month GTM
(go-to-market) launch package** — proof that Bryan and Iain can and have
sold a materially higher-priced offer to the right merchant. Relevant to
the $20k/month-each target: this may be a faster lever than pure volume
growth at $1,950.

## Diagnosis (confirmed by Bryan)

The bottleneck is **lead volume, not conversion** — "Not enough leads...I
close most leads." So the fix is getting more of the *right* leads (referral-
shaped, $5M+ Shopify Plus) in front of Bryan, not improving his close rate.
